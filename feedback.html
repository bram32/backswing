Thank you — your message is on its way.

It went without a name, an address or an identifier attached. It lands in the maker's inbox as an ordinary e-mail. Because nothing identified you there is no way to reply, so fixes and changes show up in the next release instead.

What gets sent

  • The message you type, and the category you picked.
  • The version line above, only if you tick the box.
  • Nothing else: no name, no e-mail address, no IP address, no cookies, and nothing the app has stored on your device — your plan, log, streak and settings never leave it.

Messages are passed to an e-mail relay which turns them into an e-mail to the maker of Uncoil. Uncoil is an independent app made in the Netherlands. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
