Free Relief

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Last updated 6 September 2026

Free Relief collects no personal data at all. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server that stores anything about you. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Free Relief is made and operated by Bram Pek. If you have a question about this policy, write to brampek@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, date of birth, height, weight, location or any other identifier. There is no sign-up and no login. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, fingerprinting, or any third-party SDK that reports back to us or to anyone else.

Because we never receive your information, we also never sell it, share it, or use it to build a profile of you — there is nothing to sell or share.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your progress so it is useful the next time you open it. That information is written to your browser's localStorage (or, in the iOS app, to the app's own private storage) and never leaves the device:

  • Your answers in the "Fix it" planner and the plan it produced.
  • Routines and exercises you have completed, and your day streak.
  • Entries you make in the round log, including any notes you type.
  • Preferences such as the light or dark theme.

We cannot read any of it. It is not backed up to us, not synced between your devices by us, and not transmitted anywhere. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud or to your computer), that backup is handled entirely by Apple or by you under their terms, not by us.

Health information

Some of what you type — the body area that hurts, when it hurts — is health-related. It is treated the same as everything else: stored only on your device, never transmitted, never seen by us. Free Relief does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not request access to it.

Network requests

The app is built to work fully offline, and it contacts no third party at all. Everything it needs — the 3D library (three.js), the fonts, the anatomy geometry — is bundled with it rather than fetched from a content delivery network. There is no analytics endpoint, no ad network and no tracker to block.

  • iOS app: no network requests whatsoever. Every file is inside the app, so it runs with no internet connection and has nothing to send.
  • Website: your browser downloads the app's own files from our host, Cloudflare Pages, and then caches them so later visits work offline too. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the technical request data needed to serve a page — see Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not run analytics on top of those logs and we do not read them.

Children

Free Relief is a general fitness and wellness guide intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and since it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights, and how to delete your data

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to look up or erase on our side — and no request is needed. You are always in full control of the copy on your device:

  • iOS app: delete the app. Touch and hold the Free Relief icon, choose Remove App, then Delete App. Everything the app saved is removed with it.
  • iPhone or iPad browser: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data, find the site and swipe to delete it.
  • Desktop browser: open the site, then use your browser's site settings (in Chrome: the icon to the left of the address bar → Cookies and site data → Manage on-device site data → Delete) or clear browsing data for the site.
  • Anywhere: a private or incognito window keeps nothing after you close it.

Deleting site data or the app is permanent and immediate. Your log, streak and plan cannot be recovered afterwards, by you or by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Since the app holds no contact details for you, we cannot notify you directly — please check back here.
