13°

Lumbar spine

Five big vertebrae built for bending and bracing, not turning. About 13 degrees of rotation in total, and every degree past that is borrowed from the joints and discs.

35°

Thoracic spine

Twelve vertebrae with ribs attached. This is where the turn should come from. Desk life cuts it in half, and the swing has to find the rest somewhere.

45°

Hips

Each hip should rotate inward about 45 degrees. That is the trail hip on the way back and the lead hip through impact. Stiff hips sway and slide, and the back pays.
